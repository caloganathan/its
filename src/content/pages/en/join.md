## Be part of the community

Whether you have just arrived in Indonesia or have long called it home, there is a place for you in the Sangam. Tell us how you would like to take part — as a member, a volunteer, a performer, a partner or a sponsor — and a committee member will be in touch.

Membership details, including eligibility and any applicable fees, will be published here once confirmed. Submitting an enquiry does not by itself confer membership.
