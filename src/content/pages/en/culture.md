## A living heritage

Tamil is one of the world's oldest living languages, with a literary tradition that has been continuously read, recited and celebrated for centuries. This section shares educational material and community stories about Tamil language, arts, customs and heritage — for Tamil families in Indonesia and for anyone curious to learn.

## What you will find here

- Tamil language and literature
- Festivals and their cultural significance
- Music, dance, theatre and the visual arts
- Oral histories and learning between generations
- Learning resources for families and young people
