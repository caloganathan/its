## How this website handles your information

This is a static information website. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it does not collect or store personal data. Like any website, our hosting provider processes basic technical data (such as IP addresses in server logs) to deliver and secure the site.

The registration and enquiry form prepares an email in your own email application. Nothing is sent until you choose to send that email. Information you send by email is used only to respond to your enquiry and is handled by the Sangam committee.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through the form or by email.

## Questions

For any privacy question or request, email [contact@indonesiatamilsangam.com](mailto:contact@indonesiatamilsangam.com).
