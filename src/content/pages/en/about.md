## Who we are

Indonesia Tamil Sangam is a community association that brings together Tamil families and friends living in Indonesia. We celebrate Tamil language and heritage, create opportunities to connect across generations, and build friendship with the wider Indonesian community.

## Our vision

A connected Tamil community in Indonesia that celebrates its language and heritage while building meaningful relationships across generations.

## Our mission

- Encourage the appreciation and transmission of Tamil language and cultural heritage.
- Provide opportunities for community connection and participation.
- Share reliable information about Sangam programmes and community activities.
- Collaborate with cultural, educational and community organisations.

## Registration

Indonesia Tamil Sangam is registered in Indonesia as *Persekutuan dan Perkumpulan Paguyuban Indonesia Tamil Sangam*, with its office at Citra Tower Kemayoran, Central Jakarta.
